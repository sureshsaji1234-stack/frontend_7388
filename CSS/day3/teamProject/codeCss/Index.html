Challeges Faced By Swiggy

Swiggy faces several problems in its food delivery business. One major challenge is high delivery costs, especially during peak hours and in areas with fewer orders. It also faces strong competition from platforms like Zomato. Other issues include late deliveries, customer complaints, restaurant quality problems, and delivery partner availability. Managing food quality, maintaining affordable prices, and keeping both customers and restaurants satisfied are also important challenges for Swiggy.
