The Helpful Stranger

One evening, a young boy named Ravi was walking home from school. He was carrying his school bag and thinking about the homework he had to finish. The sky was getting dark, and a cool wind was blowing.

On the way, Ravi noticed an old man standing near a bus stop. The man looked worried and was searching through his pockets. Ravi stopped and asked, “Grandpa, are you looking for something?” The old man said, “Yes, I have lost my bus ticket. I think it fell somewhere near this place.” Ravi looked around carefully. He checked near the bench and under the bus stop. After a few minutes, he saw a small piece of paper lying near a tree. He picked it up and found that it was the old man’s bus ticket.The old man was very happy.

He thanked Ravi and said, “You are a kind boy. You could have simply walked away, but you stopped to help me.” Ravi smiled and replied, “My teacher always tells us that even a small act of kindness can make someone’s day better.” The old man got on the bus and waved goodbye. Ravi continued walking home. He felt happy, not because he had received any reward, but because he had helped someone who needed him. That night, Ravi finished his homework with a smile. He understood that being kind does not require money or special skills. Sometimes, all it takes is a little time and a caring heart.

Moral of the Story

A small act of kindness can make a big difference in someone’s life.
