Roasting

One drum, one roaster, one profile per lot. We cup every batch the next morning and throw out what misses.

  1. 1. Green intake

    Sample-roasted and scored before we commit to a bag. Moisture logged at 10–12%.

  2. 2. The drum

    4 kg batches, 11 to 14 minutes, charge temperature set by density rather than by habit.

  3. 3. Rest and cup

    Two days off-gassing, then a blind table. Roast date is printed on every bag.

Brewing

Two recipes we hand out at the counter. Both assume freshly ground coffee and water just off the boil.

Steel filter decoction

20 minutes · makes 4 cups

Three heaped spoons of coarse grounds, tamped lightly. Pour 120 ml of water, cover, and leave it alone. Mix the decoction 30:70 with boiled milk and sugar to taste.

Pour-over

3 minutes · makes 1 cup

18 g coffee, 300 g water at 94°C. Bloom with 50 g for 40 seconds, then pour in two even stages. Aim to finish the drawdown by 2:45.
